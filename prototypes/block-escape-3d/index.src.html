Block Escape 3D (prototype)

Prototype for Yebee Games. Not a release build: no ads, no coins, all levels open.
  • Swipe a block to slide it. It stops at the next block or edge.
  • Blocks leave through a gate of their own colour on the cube's edges.
  • At a plain edge, swipe again to roll the block onto the next side.
  • Drag empty space to turn the cube. Undo is free.
