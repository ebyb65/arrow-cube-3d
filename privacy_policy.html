Privacy Policy for Arrow Cube 3D

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Arrow Cube 3D, developed and published by Yebee Games.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Arrow Cube 3D is designed with player privacy as our foremost priority. We do not require user registration, account creation, or login. We do not collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location.

2. Local Device Storage

Game progression, unlocked levels, player coins, powerup inventories, customized avatar emojis, and gameplay preferences are stored entirely locally on your device via standard browser local storage / sandboxed app storage. You can reset or wipe this data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

When you purchase coin packs or tactical powerup bundles within Arrow Cube 3D, all monetary transactions and payment details are processed exclusively and securely by Google Play Billing. Yebee Games does not receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or financial credentials.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support free gameplay and optional rewarded hints, the Game integrates third-party advertising services (such as Unity Ads). Third-party advertising partners may collect anonymized, non-personal diagnostic and device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and OS version) in accordance with their privacy policies to deliver contextually relevant advertisements and prevent ad fraud.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

5. Children’s Privacy

Arrow Cube 3D is a general-audience spatial puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy or games, please contact the Yebee Games team at: support@yebeegames.com.
