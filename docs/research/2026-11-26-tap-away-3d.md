# Teardown: Tap Away 3D — 26 Nov 2026

Nora, Ideas lab. Relevant to **Arrow Cube 3D** (same core idea: tap blocks on a 3D shape and they fly out in one direction; rotate to see the other sides). Also useful for Friday's game #3 review.

## Sources and limits

- Tap Away 3D (Popcore GmbH), App Store pages read 26 Nov 2026:
  - US: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tap-away-3d/id1568058543?uo=2
  - GB: https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/tap-away-3d/id1568058543
  - AU: https://apps.apple.com/au/app/tap-away-3d/id1568058543
  - CA: https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/tap-away-3d/id1568058543?at=11l4Gs
- I could not find its Google Play page through search this shift, so this is App Store data. 16 reviews in total (4 shown per storefront), dated 2021 to Nov 2026. That is a small, complaint-heavy sample, not the whole rating population. No videos or articles; nothing here comes from playing the game.

## Size and business

- Ratings: US 4.4 (~228K), GB 4.3 (~25K), CA 4.2 (~27K), AU 4.2 (~14K). Age 13+, "Contains: Advertising". Download ~469 MB.
- Developer website rollicgames.com, privacy policy on take2games.com (a large publisher, not an indie).
- IAPs (US): Remove Ads $7.99, cash packs $0.99-$59.99, "Permanent Worker" $3.99, Skip Tickets $1.99, 30 Days of Cash $3.99. GB/AU add seasonal items (Easter token booster, Halloween Battle Pass $14.99 AU).
- Update notes are the same generic line every time ("We gave things a polish..."), roughly every 1-3 weeks; version 169.x, so a long-running live game.

**Takeaway for the genre:** a 3D "tap blocks out" puzzle can hold a 4.2-4.4 rating across 200K+ ratings. The 3D cube itself is not what players complain about.

## Onboarding and level design

- Listing pitch: tap blocks to make them fly away; "Swipe to rotate the shape"; shapes get bigger and blocks change form as you progress; skins, themes, challenges; plays "offline and on the go".
- No review mentions the tutorial, lives or energy (neither praise nor complaint).
- Difficulty is praised as "challenging" (3 of 16) and "relaxing" (12 of 16): the pace is the player's own.
- Level quality problems: "Memory Challenge level 23" numbers fail to render (GB); an empty level with no way forward after a gold-block screen (AU); "level 14 yellow is hard to see" (GB).
- Readability: two reviews say block skins make the arrow direction hard to read (US, AU).

## Ads and interruptions (the main complaint)

- 15 of 16 reviews complain about ads or ad-gated rewards.
- Frequency quoted by players: "about every minute", "every 3 minutes", "after a few seconds of play", "after every level".
- **Mid-level interruptions are what hurt most.** 12 of 16 name the "gold/special blocks": a special block inside the puzzle that pauses play 5-10 seconds and offers coins for a video; several say it shows an ad even when they tap skip. One AU player: "interrupted 14 times by the golden blocks, and another 7 level-up notifications" in one level. A CA player: ads "in the MIDDLE of a level", while "ads between levels are fine".
- Level-up / rank-up / leaderboard / race pop-ups during play are named in 6 reviews; one AU player was put on a leaderboard without being asked.
- **"Remove Ads" that doesn't remove everything:** 7 of 16 paid for no-ads and still got ad prompts or interruptions. "What part of NO ADS do you not understand?" (GB, Nov 2026).
- Coins with nothing to spend on: 5 reviews ("millions of coins" with nothing to buy).
- Daily challenge has a timer with paid extra time (US).

## Retention features

- Challenges (including "Memory Challenge" levels), races, leaderboards, rank-ups, skins/themes, seasonal passes and token events.
- No review mentions streaks. The retention features that players mention are mentioned as interruptions, not as reasons to come back. The reason they give for staying is the puzzle itself ("relaxing", "addictive", "played for over a year").

## How Arrow Cube compares (checked in index.html on develop, 26 Nov)

- **Mid-level interruptions:** none from ads. Interstitials only after a win (from level 6, every 3rd win; never after a loss). Chests are a "Treasure chest earned! Tap to open" banner, not a forced pop-up; the video to open it early is optional and there's a free 4h timer. This is exactly what the Tap Away reviewers ask for. Keep it.
- **One-time tip cards** exist (e.g. "Spin the cube: Drag anywhere to rotate the cube") and are being extended (ac-intro-cards). Keep them one-time only; Tap Away's repeated level-up pop-ups show what over-messaging costs.
- **Coins have uses:** 4 themes (800-1,500 coins) and power-ups (eraser, wand). OK for now; watch that coins don't pile up with nothing left to buy once all themes are owned.
- **Theme readability** (the Tap Away skin complaint). I computed WCAG contrast from the THEMES colours:
  - Arrow vs cube on the facing side: Classic 17.9, Sunset 13.7, Crystal 8.6, Wood 7.2, Neon 4.4. All readable.
  - Dimmed arrows on sides turned away (`back` colour, drawn at 0.38 opacity): Classic 2.6, Neon 2.7, Sunset 1.7, Crystal 1.5, **Wood 1.06** (almost the same colour as the wood cube). Side-face arrows are meant to be dim, but in Wood they practically vanish, so a player rotating the cube can't see what's on the next side. Worth a look on a phone. (Backlog: ac-theme-legibility, Lena.)
- No timers, no leaderboards, no auto-enrolment in anything. Good.

## What we can learn (without copying)

1. **Never put an interruption inside a level** — not ads, not reward offers, not level-up or rank pop-ups. It's already a studio rule for ads; Tap Away's reviews show it applies just as much to non-ad pop-ups. Arrow Cube passes today; keep it as a QA check when new features land.
2. **Every theme must keep arrow directions readable**, including on side faces. One cheap check per theme (ac-theme-legibility).
3. **If we ever sell "Remove Ads", it must remove every forced ad and every ad prompt that isn't a button the player tapped**, and the store text must say exactly what it removes. Not actionable now (no IAPs yet); noted for Omar for whenever IAPs come up.
4. **Don't sign players up for leaderboards, races or events they didn't ask for.** Not relevant today; noted for future features.
5. **Broken levels get one-star reviews by number** ("level 23"). Our solvability check stays part of every level change.
6. **For game #3:** Tap Away shows a 3D tap-out puzzle can be read at a glance when the rule is one sentence ("tap a block, it flies the way it points"). The CEO's issue with Block Escape 3D was rolling over edges and gates, i.e. too many rules, not 3D itself. The one-screenshot bar still holds.

## Not copying

The name "Tap Away", its icon, its block shapes and skins, gold blocks, cash packs and battle passes stay theirs. The lessons above are generic: no mid-level interruptions, readable themes, honest no-ads wording, level quality.
