# Trader status declaration: what changed and what we need (Theo, 27 Nov 2026)

Backlog: trader (Theo). The CEO plans to declare this weekend (said 23 Nov).

## What Google's help page says (official, read 27 Nov)
Source: Play Console Help, "Trader status declaration", https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17594752?hl=en

- All active developer accounts must declare trader or non-trader (EU and UK consumer law). Existing accounts: by **1 February 2027**. Not done "may trigger enforcement including app update blocks and removal from the Play Store globally."
- A trader is anyone acting "for purposes relating to their trade, business, craft or profession". Google lists **ad-sponsored apps** as an example of a profit motive. Both our games show ads to earn money, so Yebee Games is very likely a trader. (Not legal advice; Google's page says the same.)
- A false non-trader declaration can lead to suspension or termination of the account, so "non-trader" is not a safe shortcut.
- **New: from 1 March 2027 the contact details are shown to users everywhere, not only in the EU/UK.** For an individual developer account that declares trader, "a phone number and address will now be displayed". Organization accounts show their D-U-N-S number under App support. EU/UK users also see the trader name in "Offered by".
- Traders must **give a Terms of Service link for each app** and self-certify that they only offer products that comply with EU law.
- Where: account level, Play Console > Manage account (or Settings) > Account details > About you. Per app (traders): Monitor and improve > Policy and programs > App content.
- A news report (Analytics Insight, 27 Nov 2026) says Google does not accept PO box addresses. Google's help page does not mention PO boxes, so treat that as unconfirmed.

## What it means for us
1. The address and phone the CEO enters will be public worldwide from 1 Mar 2027. He should decide now which ones (a home address, or a paid business address or virtual office, which would be spending money). Check what Play Console already shows under Account details: those may be the ones that get published.
2. Each app needs a Terms of Service link before he can finish the per-app step. We have none today. Draft: docs/compliance/terms-of-service-draft.md (this branch). It needs the CEO's read and a working contact email (the feedback Gmail, ac-feedback-addr) before it goes online; publishing it is a public step, so it is his approval.
3. Nothing changes in the games themselves.
